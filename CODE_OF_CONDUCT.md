# Code of conduct

Treat people with respect in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. Give specific technical feedback, accept corrections, and make participation possible for people with different backgrounds and experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior, personal attacks, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable. Repeated disruption or unwanted contact is also unacceptable.

If a discussion becomes hostile, stop participating and report the offending content through GitHub's reporting controls. For a project moderation request, open an issue referring to the relevant public discussion without repeating abusive material or disclosing private information. Do not post sensitive incident details publicly; request a private contact channel instead.

The maintainer, @MBuelowius, may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, warn participants, or restrict participation according to severity and repetition. These rules apply to everyone, including maintainers. Reports should be handled with discretion, and retaliation against a reporter is unacceptable.
